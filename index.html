Keyra Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Keyra is an iOS app for landlords to track properties, tenants, rent payments and expenses. This policy explains what information is stored and where.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter (properties, tenants, rent, payments, expenses, notes and attached documents) is stored only on your iPhone. Keyra has no server that collects, sees or stores this information. It is never sold or shared for advertising.

Face ID

If you turn on app lock, Face ID is handled entirely by iOS. Keyra never receives your biometric data.

Camera and photos

If you attach a lease agreement, receipt or document, Keyra asks for camera or photo access. The images are saved inside the app on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Reminders

Rent reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone; no information is sent to a server.

Backups and PDF reports

Backups and PDF reports are created on your device. They only leave your phone if you choose to share or save them yourself (for example to Files, iCloud Drive or email).

Purchases

Subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store; your payment details stay with Apple and never reach us. To verify your subscription, Keyra uses RevenueCat, which receives only a randomly generated anonymous ID and your purchase history, never your name, email or rental data. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Anonymous usage statistics

To improve the app, anonymous usage information (app opens, which screens are viewed, purchase steps, iOS and app version, language and approximate country/city) is sent to PostHog (European Union servers). It is tied only to a randomly generated ID; your name, email, tenants or financial data are never sent. Approximate location is derived from your IP address; GPS is not used. See the PostHog Privacy Policy.

Tracking and advertising

Keyra shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites. Usage statistics are not used for advertising and are never sold.

Children

Keyra is not intended for children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all Keyra data from your phone. Backup files you exported yourself remain wherever you saved them.

Support and contact

For questions or suggestions, contact the developer through Keyra's App Store page.

Gizlilik Politikası (Türkçe özet)

Keyra'ya girdiğin bütün bilgiler (mülkler, kiracılar, kira, ödemeler, giderler, belgeler) yalnızca iPhone'unda saklanır; Keyra'nın bir sunucusu yoktur. Face ID tamamen iOS tarafından yönetilir. Kamera ve fotoğraflar yalnızca belge eklemek içindir ve hiçbir yere yüklenmez. Hatırlatmalar yerel bildirimdir. Yedek ve PDF dosyaları yalnızca sen paylaşırsan telefonundan çıkar. Satın almalar Apple üzerindendir; RevenueCat'e yalnızca anonim bir kimlik ve satın alma geçmişi iletilir. Uygulamayı iyileştirmek için anonim kullanım istatistikleri (açılışlar, görüntülenen ekranlar, satın alma adımları, sürüm, dil ve IP'den çıkarılan yaklaşık ülke/şehir) rastgele bir kimlikle PostHog'a (AB sunucuları) gönderilir; adın ya da kira/finans bilgilerin gönderilmez. Reklam yok, takip yok.
